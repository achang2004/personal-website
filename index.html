About

Most small businesses don't have an operations team, a data team, or anyone whose job is to figure out what AI can actually do for them. That's the gap I fill.

I've worked as a software engineer at Google, Amazon, and TikTok, and published machine learning research at multiple machine learning conferences (you can find it on my Google Scholar). I also co-run WinTone, which helps Chinese brands break into U.S. retail, and Keystone Consulting, which helps students gain admission to top U.S. universities. In both, I've built the actual AI systems: email pipelines, automated reporting, meeting summaries, document workflows. Not demos. Production.

That's what you get when you work with me: someone who has been on both sides of the table, and who builds systems sized for a small team, not a Fortune 500 budget.
